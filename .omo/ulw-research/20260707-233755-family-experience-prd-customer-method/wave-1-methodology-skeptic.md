## Findings

- Treat the six groups as recruitment strata, not fictional personas.
- Require recent behavior: searched for a child outing in the last 30-60 days.
- Separate stated preference, observed task behavior, and source-backed feasibility.
- Use a two-stage validation funnel:
  - Stage 1: discovery screen across six groups.
  - Stage 2: task validation only for promoted groups.
- Main risks: persona fantasy, overclaim demand, false-positive survey responses, segment dilution, and unsupported feature drift.

## Proposed Six Groups

1. Infant/toddler primary caregivers: 0-2 years.
2. Preschool planners: 3-6 years.
3. Elementary enrichment planners: 7-12 years.
4. Working parents with low planning time.
5. Co-caregivers: grandparents, nannies, relatives.
6. Traveling or unfamiliar-area families.

- LEAD: 18-user discovery plus promoted task-test funnel - WHY: prevents six fake personas from becoming product requirements - ANGLE: recent-behavior parent outing search, source-caveat tolerance, Top-3 action-card task success
