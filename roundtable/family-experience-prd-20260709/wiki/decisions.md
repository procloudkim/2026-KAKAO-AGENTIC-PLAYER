# Decisions

1. Keep the product promise as bounded parent decision support in chat.
2. Keep one public MCP tool, `find_family_experiences`, through PlayMCP private smoke.
3. Treat source/cache transparency fields as core requirements.
4. Keep all unsupported claims blocked: complete nationwide coverage, real-time freshness, reservation availability, open-now status, child safety certification, guaranteed suitability, PlayMCP review completion, public release, and contest submission.
5. Prioritize deployment evidence and PlayMCP private smoke over new feature expansion.

Rejected or deferred:

- Multi-tool expansion before private smoke.
- Unofficial scraping or browser parsing.
- Broad market-ready or public-beta copy before remote and PlayMCP evidence.
- Blocking deployment on a new AI-DLC map unless the operator needs that handoff artifact.
